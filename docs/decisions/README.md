# Architecture Decision Records (ADRs)

Decisions for the payments app, numbered on their own (`001-`, `002-`, …). They follow the format and
guidelines of `seminary/docs/decisions/README.md`: short (about 100 words each for context and
consequences), written at the moment of decision, immutable once accepted.

ADRs are for contributors. NEVER mention ADRs or decision numbers in public-facing text: doctype and
field descriptions, labels, or `frappe.throw` messages.
